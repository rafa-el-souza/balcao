  • A model call opens to seven lines. The prompt line is the system prompt's size and digest, never its text (SR-12).   • The chat holds the window: beside each other, the conversation and the glass box scroll each inside its own column, so opening a row or a new row arriving never moves the composer or the totals. A new message or row scrolls its own column to itself.
  • For the visitor who stays two minutes and clicks nothing: the turn in progress and the last turn are drawn open, every call with its tokens, cache and cost in sight; earlier turns fold into one line each, with their events, time and cost, and open on demand. Closed, a call's line still carries its cost and its time.

The line opens to one line per turn, and each of those to its rows; the whole record, at full length, is the trace page. The conversation and the glass box each scroll to the newest on arrival.
