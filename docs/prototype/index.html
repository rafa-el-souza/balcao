Balcão the prototype

Static drawings of the public screens, in Portuguese, each true at 390 px and at 1280 px. The direction is the pharmacy's printed stationery: one green ink on pale green paper for the counter, a till receipt for the instrument.

Where the drawings leave the design brief, for its amendment: the counter's face is set in more than two weights; the cross beside "pensando…" pulses for as long as the turn runs; small marks drawn for this project sit on links and buttons (the pulse, the arrows, the reset, the bin, the receipt, the envelope) where the brief has no illustration; the instrument is near-black on off-white rather than ink on paper; a third face signs the author's name; and the meanings "normal" and "success" are printed in the brand's one green ink instead of a green of their own, always beside a mark that is not colour (a tick, the mode's name), while economy, pause and error keep colours that mean nothing else. The brief's one chip is drawn as two: the opener and the composer's suggestion. The calendar page (/calendar) comes with its own slice and is not drawn here.
